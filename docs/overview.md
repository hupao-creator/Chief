# Find and track tasks in Overview

## Find a task by folder or tag

Press **⌘K** to open **Overview**. Overview opens the first available folder or Inbox tag when you have no current selection. Click a folder icon at the top to see that project’s tagged work. Folders with the same name appear separately.

Tasks without a project folder belong to Inbox. Their tags appear in the same top row, each with an emoji and a name. Click a tag there to see its matching cards. A task appears once it has a tag from Tag Agent. With only untagged tasks, Overview shows **No tagged conversations yet**. Your conversations stay saved.

When a folder has tags to choose from, look for their emoji along the left edge. Move the pointer over or just beside an emoji to see its tag name. The button expands just enough to fit the name. Long names end in an ellipsis when space runs out. Click the button to show the matching cards. The selected tag, including **Any tag**, keeps its name visible when you move the pointer away.

A colored button marks your selection. A tag takes its tint from its emoji, whether you choose it at the top or on the left. Selected folders have a copper-orange tint. When **Any tag** is selected, its solid copper-orange background keeps the asterisk and name readable over the cards.

Click the asterisk (`*`) for **Any tag** to see all tagged work in the folder. Choosing another folder starts you at **Any tag**. Switching to a different folder or tag shows all statuses in that space. Selecting the same space again keeps your status filter. If a folder and its only tag show the same cards, choosing the folder is enough.

Look for tiny dots sweeping diagonally behind a folder icon or tag emoji to find a task that’s running or waiting for your response. Choose that folder or tag, then click ⏳ on the right to see its running tasks. With **Reduce Motion** turned on in macOS, the dots stay still.

A red number on a folder counts conversations with an unread completed result or Super Thread document among its tagged cards. A red number on a tag counts unread conversations in that tag. Open a card with a red bookmark to read the result or document. Its count drops in every folder or tag that includes it, and disappears when you’ve read them all.

If a Super Thread in the same view cites a task's latest run, its card can stand in for the task card. Open **Related executions** in the Super Thread to reach the task. A newer run can make the task card appear again. [See the work behind a Super Thread](reports-tags-and-changes.md#see-the-work-behind-a-super-thread)

## Filter by status

The three emoji on the right filter the cards in your current folder or tag. From top to bottom, click ⏳ for **Running only**, 🗂️ for **All statuses**, or 🏁 for **Completed only**. Move the pointer over or just beside an emoji to expand its button to the left and see the status name. Click the emoji or its name to filter the cards. The selected status keeps its name visible. Other status names appear while you hover. The selected button takes its tint from the emoji, so 🏁 has a neutral gray tint. Overview starts with 🗂️ selected.

You can also swipe up or down with two fingers over the cards or background. Start on the **left half of the window** to change tags, or the **right half** to change status. Slide your fingers up to select the choice below, or down to select the choice above. From **All statuses**, swiping up takes you to **Completed only**. One swipe moves one choice, even if the pointer crosses the middle. At either end, the selection stays put. When the left column is hidden, as it is for Inbox tags, a left-half swipe keeps your selection.

**Running only** includes tasks preparing submitted input, executing, waiting for your response, or still working in the background. **Completed only** includes tasks whose latest run finished successfully and have no pending input or background work. It also includes Super Threads and their collections. Completed task cards show when the latest run finished. Super Thread cards show when the document was last updated. Idle tasks and visible interrupted tasks with no ongoing work appear under **All statuses**.

When no cards match **Completed only**, you’ll see Chief’s skillet with its bowl centered above **No completed conversations**. The skillet gently bobs with three floating z’s. With **Reduce Motion** on, the illustration stays still. If no cards match either status filter, click 🗂️ on the right to see all statuses for the same folder or tag. With a mouse wheel, scroll up on the right half to leave an empty **Running only** view, or down there to leave an empty **Completed only** view.

## Move around the canvas

Resize the window to adjust how many cards you can see. Overview arranges cards across the window and centers them vertically. The folder and tag buttons at the top have a frosted glass background. The clear glass buttons on the left and right float over the cards. With **Reduce transparency** turned on in macOS, the filters have a solid background. Click a filter to select it, or click the visible part of a card between filters to open it. A wide task card can appear between two taller cards when there’s room for it there. Opening the Chief input box keeps the cards in place.

With the input box closed, swipe up over the eyes on Chief Bar to open **Chief Note**. Scroll inside an open note or a long Chief Bar message to read more.

When the cards extend beyond the right edge, swipe left or right with two fingers on a trackpad, or turn the mouse wheel. With keyboard focus on a card, use the Left and Right Arrow keys to scroll. In **Completed only**, an outline marks the task card with keyboard focus. Click a task card, or press Return on a focused task, to open it.

Overview keeps your place when you close a conversation or Settings. If you widen the window or choose a filter so all the cards fit, Overview centers them. Horizontal swipes and the mouse wheel leave a fitting grid centered. If some columns are still offscreen, you can scroll to the last one.

If the cards in your current view would extend past the window, Overview can group Super Threads into a collection. Widen the window or choose a folder or tag with fewer cards. Super Threads reappear as individual cards as space opens up. Reports you haven’t visited for a day can stay grouped. Click a collection’s page count to browse its members. [Browse Super Threads in a collection](reports-tags-and-changes.md#browse-super-threads-in-a-collection)

## Check progress or respond

Click a task card to open its conversation. Cards show statuses such as **Running**, **Completed**, and **Interrupted**. If a card shows a question or permission request, respond on the card or open the conversation for context. **Background work is still running** means work continues after the agent has replied.

While a task is running, a band of tiny dots sweeps diagonally behind its emoji in the harness color. With **Reduce Motion** turned on in macOS, the dots stay still. When the task completes, a check mark appears on the badge.

A red bookmark on the upper-left edge of the emoji badge means there’s a completed result or Super Thread document you haven’t viewed. Open the card and let the result or document appear in the foreground. When you return to Overview, the bookmark is gone. Chief remembers this after you quit. A newly completed run or a new version of the document brings the bookmark back.

Overview hides a task conversation if its latest run failed or Chief stopped it. You may still reach it from an existing link in a Chief note or Super Thread. To send a follow-up, open the conversation and use its input box.

## Archive a conversation

After you stop a task yourself, you can archive its conversation once it has no input waiting to send and no background work. Hover over the card and click the close icon that replaces the emoji. You can also focus **Archive conversation** and press Return. The card disappears from Overview and the saved conversation stays on your Mac. If archiving fails, the card shows an error and you can retry there. Overview and Settings have no restore list for archived conversations.

Super Threads have a separate archive action that also hides their source conversations. [Archive a Super Thread](reports-tags-and-changes.md#archive-a-super-thread)

When the workspace has no cards left, Chief opens its input box as the last card fades away.
