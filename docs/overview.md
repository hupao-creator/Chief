# Find and track tasks in Overview

## Find a task by folder or tag

Press **⌘K** to open **Overview**. In the filter bar at the top center, select **All** to see the full view, or click a project folder to see its tasks. If the bar wraps to a second row, look below the window controls. Move the pointer over a folder with tags to pick one of its tags. Tasks with no project folder go into Inbox. Their tags appear directly in the filter bar.

Folders with the same name appear separately. If a folder and its only tag show the same cards, select the folder. If your filter seems too narrow, choose **All**.

If a Super Thread in the same view cites a task's latest run, its card can stand in for the task card. Open **Related executions** in the Super Thread to reach the task. A newer run can make the task card appear again. [See the work behind a Super Thread](reports-tags-and-changes.md#see-the-work-behind-a-super-thread)

## Move around the canvas

Resize the window to adjust how many cards you can see. Overview arranges the cards automatically and centers them vertically. A wide task card can appear between two taller cards when there’s room for it there. In windows taller than 60 points, the cards have at least 30 points of space above and below them. The filter bar and Chief Bar float above the cards. Opening the Chief input box keeps the cards in place.

When the cards extend beyond the right edge, swipe left or right with two fingers on a trackpad, or turn the mouse wheel. With keyboard focus on a card, use the Left and Right Arrow keys to scroll. Click a task card, or press Return on a focused task, to open it.

Overview keeps your place when you close a conversation or Settings. If you widen the window or choose a filter so all the cards fit, Overview centers them. They stay in place when you swipe or use the scroll wheel. If some columns are still offscreen, you can scroll to the last one.

If the cards in your current view would extend past the window, Overview can group Super Threads into a collection. Widen the window or choose a folder or tag with fewer cards. Super Threads reappear as individual cards as space opens up. Reports you haven’t visited for a day can stay grouped. Click a collection’s page count to browse its members. [Browse Super Threads in a collection](reports-tags-and-changes.md#browse-super-threads-in-a-collection)

## Check progress or respond

Click a task card to open its conversation. Cards show statuses such as **Running**, **Completed**, and **Interrupted**. If a card shows a question or permission request, respond on the card or open the conversation for context. **Background work is still running** means work continues after the agent has replied.

A blue bookmark on the upper-left edge of the emoji badge means there’s a completed result or Super Thread document you haven’t viewed. Open the card and let the result or document appear in the foreground. When you return to Overview, the bookmark is gone. Chief remembers this after you quit. A newly completed run or a new version of the document brings the bookmark back.

Overview hides a task conversation if its latest run failed or Chief stopped it. You may still reach it from an existing link in a Chief note or Super Thread. To send a follow-up, open the conversation and use its input box.

## Archive a conversation

After you stop a task yourself, you can archive its conversation once it has no input waiting to send and no background work. Hover over the card and click the close icon that replaces the emoji. You can also focus **Archive conversation** and press Return. The card disappears from Overview and the saved conversation stays on your Mac. If archiving fails, the card shows an error and you can retry there. Overview and Settings have no restore list for archived conversations.

Super Threads have a separate archive action that also hides their source conversations. [Archive a Super Thread](reports-tags-and-changes.md#archive-a-super-thread)

When the workspace has no cards left, Chief opens its input box as the last card fades away.
