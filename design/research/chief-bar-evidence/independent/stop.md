# Chief Stop window and input measurements

The running Chief's Stop square has a 120 ms reveal and hide. Its Button owns activation and accessibility throughout the transition. The native-window study at `44fec23d4c59200821ae5be23fb395f8294c13cd` pins the collectors and the evidence below. Each compressed receipt identifies its own measured source and cases.

- The reduced-motion study compared 58 reveal/hide and 71 interrupted poses against the SwiftUI control. All sampled Stop pixels matched. A separate 58-pose grid with animation disabled reached the settled value on each leg. The receipt indexes the lossless frames. [Measurements](stop-motion.json.gz) · [Frames](stop-motion.mkv)
- In eight paired visits with reduced motion and the Stop centre aligned under the pointer on the display, both versions delivered one local click and one external AXPress on every visit. The disabled click was ignored and the named button disappeared on retirement. [On-display pointer and AX receipts](stop-pointer-aligned.json.gz)
- The partly off-display fixture delivered the queued click in seven of eight control visits and five of eight layer-backed visits. All sixteen received the mouse events and AXPress. These captures measure local AppKit event routing. Physical mouse hardware was outside their scope. [Off-display pointer and AX receipts](stop-pointer.json.gz)
